ABOUT US

The Kousins are a worldwide network of Kreatives whose initiative is to research and develop The Kousins Kreative Index: A worldwide resource for Arts Education and Development within public education, cultural institutions, and business. This creative industry research-led project aims to produce a work-based platform of Arts educators known as the Kousins by asking, 'How can we use kinship as a methodology for building sustainable resources for Arts education in South Africa?' Forming part of a greater ongoing cultural exchange program, Decolonial Futures a cultural exchange program organized as a collaboration of the Sandberg Instituut, the Gerrit Rietveld Academie, Framer Framed, as well as Funda Community College in Soweto, South Africa.

To build a local movement rooted in kinship, where art flourishes in historically and presently disadvantaged communities in South Africa - nurturing young creators, reclaiming imagination as a birthright, and transforming forgotten spaces into cultural hubs.

To connect artists, educators, and communities to create cultural collaborations, share resources, and foster access and kinship in historically and currently disadvantaged areas worldwide.

An Artist has to be true to their environment.
